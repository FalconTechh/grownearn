GrowNEarn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026  •  Contact: dineshdxb9583@gmail.com

Developer / data controller: Dinesh Kumar Giri (individual developer), India. Contact: dineshdxb9583@gmail.com

This policy explains what GrowNEarn ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID from Google Sign-In.
  • App activity: points, coins, streak, missions, quiz and lesson progress, referral code, leaderboard name/avatar, purchases made with in-app coins, and history of these actions.
  • Redeem details: the UPI ID, mobile number or email you type when you request a reward, used only to process that request.
  • Public profile (Chat & People): display name, avatar, optional bio and city (text you write, not GPS), blue-tick status, and "active now / last seen" time. You can hide last seen and turn off chat requests in Social settings.
  • Social content: follows, blocks, chat requests, chat messages, gifts you send or receive, and reports. Messages are visible to the person you chat with and, if a message is reported, to our moderators.
  • Blue-tick application: the category, link, reason and follower numbers you submit.
  • Support: messages you send through in-app support tickets.
  • Approximate location (optional, only if you turn on "Nearby"): we convert your phone's approximate location into a short area code that covers roughly 5 km and store only that code with your public profile, so other users can see how far away you are (as a range such as "Within 5 km"). We never store or share your exact coordinates or GPS track. Turning Nearby off deletes the area code immediately. Location permission is requested only when you enable Nearby.
  • Creator Feed & Season: posts, links, tags, likes and comments you make (public to other users), and your monthly Season points and rank (shown with your display name and avatar on the Season leaderboard).
  • Quiz Battle: your display name, avatar, answers, scores and results of duels, visible to your opponent. Matchmaking entries are temporary.
  • Chat themes and shop items: which cosmetic items you own and the theme chosen for a chat (visible to the other person in that chat).
  • Step count: read from your phone's step-counter sensor (Activity recognition permission) to calculate Walk & Earn rewards. Counting can continue in the background. Raw sensor data stays on your device; only the daily total you claim is sent to us. We do not use your location for step counting.
  • Device and ads: advertising ID, device model/OS, approximate location from IP (by Google), crash logs, analytics events and a push-notification token.
  • Images: thumbnails you choose in Growth Studio are analysed on your device and are not uploaded.

2. How we use it

To run the app (login, points, rewards, chat, gifts), prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, moderate reports, show ads, send reminders and notifications, provide support and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Sharing

  • Other users: your public profile details and the messages you send.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check) and Google AdMob. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Legal: where required by law or to protect users and the service.

4. Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob and may use the advertising ID for personalised or non-personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings. Rewarded ads are optional.

5. Permissions

  • Activity recognition: step counting for Walk & Earn (optional).
  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): only for the optional Nearby feature; you can deny it and still use the whole app.
  • Notifications: reminders and chat alerts (optional).
  • Internet: required to use the app.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, follows, blocks, your area code, and the messages you sent. Feed posts and comments, Season and battle records linked to you may take a short time to be removed or are anonymised. Records needed for fraud prevention, accounting or legal reasons (for example redeem records and reports) may be kept as required. You can also email dineshdxb9583@gmail.com to request access or deletion; we will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase, encrypted in transit, and protected by access rules. No system is 100% secure.

8. Children

GrowNEarn is for users aged 18 and above and is not directed to children. Chat requires accepting 18+ rules. If we learn a child's data was collected, we delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant changes will be shown in the app.

10. Reporting and moderation

Chat, Feed posts and comments have Report and Block options. We review reports and may remove content or accounts. User-generated content is public to other users of the app.
